About Us

Who We Are

As a pioneer in Indonesian grading, EGS offers advanced technology and slab quality comparable to global standards. Every slab is equipped with advanced anti-counterfeit holograms to safeguard your card from environmental damage over time. Each graded card is securely registered in the EGS database and can be easily verified by scanning the QR code on the slab, ensuring authenticity, traceability, and peace of mind for collectors.
